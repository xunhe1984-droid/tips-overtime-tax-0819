# Tips & Overtime Tax Exemption Calculator (2026 IRS Schedule 1-A)

A calculator for US hourly and tipped workers to calculate their exact federal and state take-home pay boost under the "No Tax on Tips & Overtime" legislation (OBBBA).

## Features
- **IRS Schedule 1-A Rules**: Calculates deductions with caps ($25,000 for tips, $12,500 for overtime).
- **Phase-Out Calculation**: Automatic reduction rules based on Modified AGI ($150,000 single / $300,000 joint).
- **50-State Conformity**: Supports state-specific rolling conformity and decouple rules.
- **Interactive Breakdown**: Weekly / Monthly / Annual take-home pay comparison.
